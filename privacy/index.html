Mortgage X Ray is a personal website operated by Raymond. F, Mortgage agent (Level 1), FSRA Lic. M26000144, with Centum Financial Services Limited Partnership, FSRA Brokerage Lic. 13054. This website currently accepts Ontario enquiries only.

What the form collects

First and last name, phone number and/or email, the property province, your mortgage goal, the originating offer or allowlisted ad creative when available, and your permission to be contacted about this enquiry. Phone or email is enough. We do not ask for a SIN, documents, account numbers or a credit check here.

When information is sent

Unsubmitted answers stay in memory in this tab and clear when you refresh or close it. When you press Send my request, the form sends the enquiry to our server on Vercel. Make.com delivers it to Zoho CRM. Confirmation appears only after delivery is acknowledged. A request reference and CRM receipt are stored in Make to prevent duplicate submissions. An uncertain delivery may already have reached the CRM; the form uses the same reference when you retry unchanged answers.

How we use it

Raymond uses your information to respond by phone or email, discuss your goal and organize follow-up. Your enquiry consent is not a marketing subscription. Your information is not sold or rented. A formal application and any later lender sharing use the brokerage’s separate process and consents.

Service providers

  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process technical request information such as IP addresses.
  • Vercel validates the enquiry and processes technical request information to operate and protect the service.
  • Make.com routes the enquiry and stores the delivery reference and receipt.
  • Zoho CRM stores your enquiry and follow-up record in Raymond’s existing mortgage enquiry system.
  • Resend sends an internal alert containing the enquiry details to Raymond’s business mailbox, hosted by Google Workspace. Alerts currently go to raymond@themortgageroom.ca and identify the enquiry as Mortgage X Ray.
  • Calendly handles scheduling when you choose a booking link. It receives technical information when opened and the name, email, phone and time you provide when booking. Submitting the website form does not book a call.

Providers may process information outside Canada under their own privacy practices. This form does not send answers to Tally. Fonts are served from the website; no Meta Pixel or advertising analytics has been enabled as part of this launch.

The separate secure application

After your short enquiry is received, you may continue to a separate mortgage application. Your enquiry and contact details remain saved even if you do not finish the application. A new enquiry opens its own application directly in a private session. To return later, use the private link sent to your email; there is no code to type. Each co-applicant uses their own emailed private link and signs separately. Keep these links private.

The application may include identification, address and employment history, income, assets, debts, mortgage details and a drawn signature. Your SIN is optional. Please use only the private application for these details, and never put them in the website chat, short enquiry or ordinary email.

The application server runs on Vercel. Encrypted drafts and access records are stored in our Canadian Supabase database. Identification images and the signed application PDF are attached to your restricted Zoho CRM record. Encrypted recovery copies are also kept with that record. Resend delivers access emails and a separate completion notice to Raymond, without attaching your application or ID files. Application contents are not sent to the chat assistant or advertising trackers.

Saved progress remains available through the email and reference on your invitation. Contact Raymond to correct a submitted application, replace a document or ask about access and retention. Application records follow the brokerage’s record-keeping policy and the consent presented before signing. Service providers may process technical information outside Canada under their own privacy practices.

Retention and your choices

The shared enquiry system keeps enquiry records for up to 24 months after the last contact. If you proceed with an application, the brokerage’s applicable record-keeping requirements apply. You may request access, correction or deletion, subject to required retention, by contacting Raymond. You may withdraw permission for further enquiry follow-up.

Contact and sensitive information

For privacy questions or requests, email raymond@themortgageroom.ca or call (416) 898-0181. Please do not email SINs, banking credentials, identity documents or full financial records. If documents are required, Raymond will explain the brokerage’s approved secure process.

This website provides general information, not an approval, offer to lend or guarantee. The first enquiry is not a mortgage application.
