Privacy notice

This notice explains how personal information is handled when you ask for a Mortgage X-Ray through this site. It covers enquiries only, not full mortgage applications.

Who is responsible

X-Ray Mortgages is the name Raymond. F, Mortgage Associate, RECA Licence [PENDING], uses for mortgage services in Alberta. Raymond. F is licensed with Centum Financial Services Limited Partnership, which is accountable for personal information collected in the course of mortgage brokerage activity. Personal information is handled in line with Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What is collected

  • Your name and how to reach you (email address and, if you choose, a phone number).
  • The goal you describe, such as a renewal, a refinance or a purchase, and the general situation you choose to share.
  • Anything you tell us on the call that you want reflected in your X-Ray.

Please do not send documents, your SIN, account numbers or income figures through the form or by email. None are needed to start.

Why it is collected

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call.
  • To prepare your one-page Mortgage X-Ray.
  • To keep a record of the enquiry where brokerage recordkeeping rules require it.

Your information is not sold, and it is not used for unrelated marketing. No credit check is done at the enquiry stage.

Service providers

The enquiry form and the booking calendar are run by third-party service providers. They process your information on our behalf to deliver the form and schedule the call, and may store it on servers outside Alberta or outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of that jurisdiction. This site uses no tracking pixels or advertising cookies.

Consent

By submitting the form or booking a call, you consent to the collection and use described here. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, subject to legal and recordkeeping requirements. If you later decide to make a full application, you will be given separate consent terms before any credit check or lender submission.

How long it is kept

Enquiry information is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above and for the period required by applicable law and brokerage policy, then securely destroyed.

Access, correction and questions

You can ask to see or correct the personal information held about you, or ask a question about this notice, by writing to [XRAY_EMAIL]. If a concern is not resolved, you may contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.
