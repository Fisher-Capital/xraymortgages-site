YOUR MORTGAGE X-RAY

What we look at.
Why it matters.

Start with a few questions. Then talk with Ray about the details that matter to you.

  1. 01

    The payment today

    List what is owed, the interest rates and what leaves your account each month.

  2. 02

    The cost over time

    A lower payment can come from a longer repayment period. Include mortgage penalties, setup fees and total interest in the comparison.

  3. 03

    The security behind it

    Moving unsecured debt onto your home changes the risk. We look at the trade-offs as well as the possible breathing room.

The important part.

Borrowing against your home puts it at risk if you cannot make payments. Consolidation does not erase debt and may increase the total interest paid.
