Know your options

Reverse mortgages, explained simply.

A reverse mortgage may let eligible homeowners aged 55 or older borrow against their home without regular mortgage payments. Interest is added to the balance, so the amount owed grows. Raymond can help you compare the costs, conditions and other options before deciding.

Use some home equity

Eligibility depends on age, property, location and lender rules. Being 55+ does not automatically qualify a home or borrower.

Understand the balance

Regular mortgage payments are generally not required while conditions are met. Interest accumulates and the loan balance grows.

Plan for repayment

Review when the loan comes due, early-repayment charges and what it could mean for your future plans and estate.

Interest increases the loan balance and can reduce remaining home equity. Property taxes, insurance, maintenance and loan conditions still apply. Repayment is generally due after sale, moving out, the last borrower’s death or default, subject to the contract. Eligibility depends on the lender, borrowers and property.

Questions you might have.

Does no regular mortgage payment mean no costs?

No. Interest accumulates, fees may apply and you must meet the lender’s conditions. Property taxes, insurance and maintenance remain your responsibility.

When must the loan be repaid?

Common triggers include selling, moving out, the last borrower’s death or default. The agreement sets the timing and conditions. Early repayment may incur charges.

Do all Alberta properties qualify?

No. Each lender has location and property rules, as well as borrower eligibility requirements. Province-wide advertising does not mean every property qualifies.

Should I compare other options?

Yes. Review a HELOC, refinancing, using other resources or downsizing where relevant. Consider independent legal and financial advice and the effect on your estate.
