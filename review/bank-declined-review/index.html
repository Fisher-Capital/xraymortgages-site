Know your options

Bank-declined mortgage review, explained simply.

A mortgage decline is a reason to look at the file more closely. Credit history, income documents, debt payments and the property can each affect a lender’s decision. We can review what happened and whether another option may fit.

Know when to wait

Sometimes the useful next step is improving the file or changing the plan, rather than another application.

A bank decline does not mean another lender will approve the application. Another application can involve a credit check and different costs. Some situations need changes before borrowing is suitable.

Questions you might have.

Does a bank decline mean every lender will decline?

Not necessarily. Lenders have different programs and criteria. Whether another route is available depends on the reason for the decline and the full application.

Can I be approved with no income or poor credit?

There is no automatic approval. We need to understand the circumstances and which, if any, lender criteria could fit. We will not promise funding based only on a credit label or home equity.

Should I apply to several lenders immediately?

Start by understanding the decline. A new application may involve a hard credit check. Compare the plan and possible costs before applying again.

What happens after I contact Ray?

Ray reviews your situation and explains the next steps. If documents are needed later, he will explain how to provide them securely. This first step is not an approval.
