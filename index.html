Questions, answered plainly.

Does self-employed income mean a private mortgage?

No. Self-employed does not mean private. Banks, credit unions and other lenders each have their own approaches for business owners. Which ones may be worth exploring depends on how the business earns, how the owner is paid and how that income is documented, and that is what a review looks at. Private financing is considered only where it is appropriate, and any private option is set out with its full costs in writing first.

Can home equity be used without replacing the current mortgage?

Sometimes. Depending on the lender and how the current mortgage is registered, options can include a line of credit or a second mortgage that sits behind the existing one, depending on your existing mortgage terms. Each option has its own costs and conditions, and whether one is available and suitable also depends on the property and your other obligations. A review sets out which options exist in a situation like yours and what each would involve. It does not promise a specific product.

What should I understand about a private mortgage?

A private mortgage is usually short term, carries a higher rate than a bank mortgage, and comes with fees. Look at the total cost, the payments, the length of the term and the conditions for renewal or early repayment. The plan to move out of it matters as much as the mortgage itself, and a planned refinance or sale can be delayed or fall through. If a private mortgage is ever an option worth considering, the brokerage sets out the full costs in writing first, before anything is signed.

Will the first conversation affect my credit?

No. The first conversation and the X-Ray involve no credit check. A credit check only happens later, if you choose to make a full application, and only with your consent.

Should I send documents now?

No. Please do not send documents, your SIN, account details, identity documents or income figures through the form, by email or by text. The X-Ray starts with a conversation. If you later choose to apply, you will be told exactly what is needed and how to send it through the brokerage's secure process.
