BEFORE WE TALK

Good questions.
Clear answers.

What is a mortgage X-ray?

A closer look at your income, debts and home plans. Ray helps you understand the details and what to consider next.

Is the first review free?

Yes. The initial conversation and one-page review are free. If you later consider a mortgage option with fees, those costs should be explained before you decide to proceed.

Will this affect my credit?

The initial review involves no credit check. Any later credit check requires your consent through the brokerage's application process.

Does self-employed mean a private mortgage?

No. Banks, credit unions and other lenders have different approaches to self-employed income. The right options depend on your circumstances and supporting records. A review helps identify what is worth exploring.

Can a smaller payment cost more overall?

Yes. Extending the time you take to repay can increase total interest, even with a smaller payment. Fees and mortgage penalties can also affect the comparison. That is why we look at the full cost.

Should I send documents now?

No. Do not send your SIN, account numbers, identity documents or financial documents by email or through this preview. If you choose to apply later, the brokerage will explain its secure process.
